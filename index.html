## Course

You can also write * **[Entity] Student** or * **[Junction Table] Enrollment** followed by attribute bullets. Key/link emoji, parenthesized PK, and FK → Student.student_id are supported. Relationship prose is ignored; foreign keys define the connections.

03 / Many-to-many relationships

Connect Student and Course through Enrollment. Its two foreign keys create two 1:N relationships. Use a composite primary key on the two foreign keys, or add a UNIQUE constraint on the pair in your database to prevent duplicate enrollments. The diagram shows maximum cardinality; nullability and unique foreign keys do not change the 1:N connector.
